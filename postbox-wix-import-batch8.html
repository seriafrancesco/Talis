PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 September 2026.

PostBox is local-first. Core capture, on-device OCR and deterministic note processing can be used without an account and do not send captured content to a paid or remote AI provider.

Data processed on your device

Captures, imported text or URLs, selected images and videos, OCR text, structured notes, edits, checklist state and local app metadata are stored on your device. Image OCR runs on-device.

Optional account and private cloud backup

If you create an account, Supabase processes your email address and authentication/session data. PostBox can back up capture metadata, notes and private media so signed-in users can restore their own data. Database row-level security and private Storage paths restrict account data to its owner.

Support and deletion requests

If you submit a public support, privacy or account-deletion request, PostBox stores the email address, request type, message and timestamps you submit. Open requests are retained while needed to handle the request. Resolved or rejected requests are automatically pruned after 365 days.

Reliability diagnostics

PostBox has no advertising SDK and no behavioral analytics. Optional reliability diagnostics contain only a coarse allowlisted error code, platform and app version. They exclude captures, URLs, filenames, notes, OCR text, transcripts, tokens, email and user identifiers, and are retained for at most 30 days.

Sharing and processors

PostBox does not sell personal data or use it for cross-app tracking. Supabase is used for optional authentication, private backup, account deletion, reliability event storage and the public support request inbox.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete a capture, erase the local archive, clear app data or uninstall the app. Optional cloud records remain until you delete them or delete your account. In-app account deletion is designed to remove the authenticated account, its database rows and private backed-up media.

Your choices

You can use PostBox without an account, disable reliability diagnostics, remove captures, erase the local archive, sign out, delete your cloud account, or submit a privacy/deletion request. Requests that require access to account data require reasonable ownership verification.

ACCOUNT DELETION

Request account deletion

If you still have the app, the fastest route is Settings → Delete account. This public process exists so deletion can also be requested without reinstalling PostBox.

A verified account deletion removes the PostBox Auth account, associated PostBox database records and private cloud media. Local files on devices that are offline or unavailable cannot be remotely erased; erasing the local archive, clearing app data or uninstalling PostBox removes those local copies.

To protect accounts, a web request is not executed from an email address alone. Ownership is verified before destructive action. Verified requests are targeted for completion within 30 days, usually sooner.
