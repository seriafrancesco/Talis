PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 September 2026.

PostBox is local-first. Core capture, on-device OCR and deterministic note organization work without an account and captured content is not sent to a metered AI service.

Data on your device

Captures, text or URLs, selected images/videos, OCR text, structured notes, edits, checklist state and local metadata are stored on-device until you delete them, erase the archive, clear app data or uninstall PostBox.

Optional account and private backup

If you create an account, Supabase processes your email and authentication/session data. Signed-in users can back up capture metadata, notes and private media. Row-level security and private Storage paths restrict account data to its owner.

Reliability diagnostics

PostBox can send only a fixed coarse error code, platform family and app version. Captures, URLs, filenames, notes, OCR text, transcripts, account identifiers, tokens and email addresses are not telemetry fields. You can disable diagnostics in Settings. Accepted events are retained up to 30 days.

Support/deletion requests

The public support/deletion forms store the email, request type, optional message and timestamps you submit for support, ownership verification, deletion and abuse prevention. Resolved request records are normally removed within 90 days unless longer retention is reasonably required for security, disputes or law.

Sharing, retention and deletion

PostBox does not sell personal data and contains no advertising SDK. Supabase is the backend processor for optional Auth, private backup, account deletion, diagnostics and support intake. In-app account deletion removes the authenticated account, associated database rows and private cloud media. You can also request deletion publicly without reinstalling the app.

Access, export and rights

Your library remains visible inside the app. For access/export or other applicable privacy-rights requests concerning cloud/support records, use Support; ownership is verified when necessary.

Children, security and changes

PostBox is a general-purpose tool, not specifically for children or specialized health, biometric, financial-account or government-identity data. It uses private Storage, RLS, authenticated destructive endpoints and no client service-role credential. Material data-flow changes require updated policy/store disclosures.

TERMS

Terms of Use

Effective 1 September 2026.

PostBox helps you capture, store, organize and edit your own reference material. Core capture and deterministic note processing run locally. Optional account features provide private cloud backup and restore.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for the material you choose to capture, including having the right to store it. Do not use PostBox to violate law, intellectual-property rights, privacy rights, platform rules or another person's rights.

Extraction accuracy

OCR, metadata parsing and deterministic organization can be incomplete or inaccurate. Keep original evidence when accuracy matters and verify important information before relying on extracted or organized text.

Availability and backups

No software or storage system is guaranteed to be uninterrupted or lossless. Optional cloud backup is a convenience, not a substitute for backups required for critical records.

Accounts and acceptable use

Accounts are optional. Keep access to your email and credentials secure. Do not use public forms for spam, automated flooding, harassment or disruption. Requests may be rate-limited when reasonably necessary to protect the service.

Consumer rights

Nothing in these terms limits rights that cannot legally be waived under applicable consumer or data-protection law.

SUPPORT

Support

Use this form for technical help or privacy requests. Do not include passwords, authentication tokens, full capture contents or other secrets.

Support and privacy requests are targeted for review within 7 days.

ACCOUNT DELETION

Request account deletion

If you still have PostBox installed, the fastest path is Settings → Delete account. This public process exists so you can request deletion without reinstalling the app.

A verified deletion removes the PostBox Auth account, associated PostBox database rows and private cloud media. Local files on devices that are offline or no longer available cannot be remotely erased; uninstalling PostBox or using Erase local archive removes local copies. To protect accounts, a web request is not executed from an email address alone. Ownership is verified before destructive action.

Verified account-deletion requests are targeted for completion within 30 days, usually sooner.
